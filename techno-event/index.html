Info

Is there a door policy?

Yes. We are building a respectful, inclusive dancefloor. Come to dance, not to watch. Our door team has the final say.

Can I take photos?

Phone cameras are stickered at the door. What happens on the floor stays on the floor.

Is there re-entry?

One re-entry until 04:00 with your wristband. After that, once you leave, you're out.

Is the venue accessible?

Hall A is step-free with an accessible toilet. Hall B is reached by a ramp. Email us ahead of time and we will arrange a companion ticket and priority entry.

Are there awareness and harm-reduction services?

Yes. Our awareness team wears orange vests and is on site all night, plus free water stations and a quiet chill-out zone.
